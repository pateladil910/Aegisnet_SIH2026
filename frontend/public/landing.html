Core Capabilities

Engineered for Extreme Environments

Tested for field deployments with solar energy harvesting and weatherized enclosures.

🌊

Flash Flood Wave Front Tracking

Upstream river catchment sensors calculate velocity vectors and rate-of-rise to alert downstream bridges and low-lying settlements with critical advance notice.

🔥

Wildfire Flame Co-Validation

Eliminates false alarms from ambient sunlight or dust by cross-validating IR spectral flickering against abrupt ambient temperature jumps.

🔋

Solar Autonomous Operation

Integrated MPPT solar management and LiFePO4 battery banks ensure 24/7 continuous operation even through monsoon cloud cover.

🗺️

Spatial IDW Correlation

Inverse Distance Weighting correlates multiple neighboring sensor nodes to distinguish local anomalies from widespread regional disasters.

📲

Multilingual Civilian SMS

Automatically formats and dispatches geo-targeted emergency warning alerts in Hindi and English via cellular SMS gateways.

📡

Decentralized Self-Healing Mesh

If any gateway or node is destroyed or submerged, surrounding nodes automatically discover alternate routing hops to preserve telemetry flow.
